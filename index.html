CONIGLIOVERDE

Coniglioverde is inspired by Eduardo Kac’s Alba project.

It is a multimedia exhibition space and a research laboratory on hybridisation between poetics and technologies.

In the time of half-realities, of virtual worlds, of AIs that can imitate thought, we seek our way to reconnect with words.

Experience Neutropoli:
a text, an underground, a pain, a silence, a vertigo, a voice, all the voices, thoughts.



Moving images have multiplied and fragmented on screens of all kinds, out and inside ourselves.

Butterfly is an immersive movie counting about a new form of life emerging from a primordial digital broth.



In the flow of time images flow, but some of them remain entangled, they stick to the gaze.

Visiting this gallery you can find projects realized with AI, generative software, sensors.

Old and new tools to explore the relationships between visible and imaginary.

We produce an ever-growing amount of data, until — over time — we ourselves become its product.

Experience H.E.Arts:
a sensitive device that records emotional art experiences.
