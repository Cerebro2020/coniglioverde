CONIGLIO VERDE

Coniglio Verde is inspired by Eduardo Kac’s Alba project.

Is a multimedia exhibition space and a research laboratory on hybridation beetween poetics and technologies.

In the time of half-realities, of virtual worlds, of AIs that can imitate thought, we seek our way to reconnect with words.

Expreince Neutropoli:
a text, an underground, a pain, a silence, a vertigo, a voice, all the voices, thoughts.



In the flow of time images flow, but some of them remain entangled, they stick to the gaze.

Visiting this gallery you can find projects realized with, AI, generative software, sensors.

Old and new tools to explore the relationships between visible and imaginary.
